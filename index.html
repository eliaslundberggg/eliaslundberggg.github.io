About Greg

A coach who still races at the front

I'm a former Cal swimmer, NCAA champion and professional triathlete. I led out the swim at the 2024 IRONMAN 70.3 World Championships, and I've coached swimmers my whole life.

I coach with a relaxed, collaborative style built on a growth mindset. I want my athletes to become true students of the sport: curious, engaged and always learning.

Success isn't only about results. It's about enjoying the process, being disciplined in the small things, and building habits, confidence and respect for the sport, with a few laughs along the way.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you work alongside my current triathlon coach?

Yes. Swim Coaching is designed to work collaboratively with your existing triathlon coach, so your swim fits into your overall training load.

How do I receive a workout plan after buying it?

After secure checkout with Stripe you receive your plan as a PDF by email, including workouts, drill demonstrations, an intensity guide and the workout theme.

Which plan should I start with?

Newer to structured swimming? Start with Technique Improvement. Racing a 70.3 soon? Pick the 3 Week 70.3 Swim Block. Still unsure? Send me a message.

Do the plans work in yards and meters?

Yes. Workouts can be swum in a yard or meter pool. Just keep the same intervals and effort.
